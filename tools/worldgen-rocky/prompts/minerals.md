# Minerals (resource mask, channel-packed, rocky only)

- R: iron / mafic ores — `#000000` none … `#FF0000` rich
- G: rare / felsic / high-value veins — `#000000` … `#00FF00`
- B: volatiles / salts / sulfur near vents and playas — `#000000` … `#0000FF`

Background rock = `#000000`. Deposits follow geology: iron in basalt and
craters, rares in highlands/veins, volatiles near vents and dry lakebeds.
Keep blobs compact and sparse; density is gameplay data, not decoration.
