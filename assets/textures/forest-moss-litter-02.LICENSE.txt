forest-moss-litter-02.bin.gz packs the native 2048x2048 RGB8 diffuse, R8
roughness and R16 displacement maps. tools/worldgen-rocky/import_moss_detail.py
reproduces it after verifying each source SHA-256. Pillow and NumPy are used
only by the offline importer. Source maps and packed bytes are identified in
forest-moss-litter-02.provenance.json.

The 3 m swatch width, 20 mm displacement range and linear RGB grade are authored
rendering priors for fictional terrain, not measured geology or moss biology.

The companion forest-moss-litter-02.normal.bin.gz contains CC0 OpenGL normals from the
same Poly Haven scan. Exact URLs, checksums and processing are in
forest-moss-litter-02.normal.provenance.json. Reproduce with
tools/worldgen-rocky/import_ground_normals.py. The old color/roughness/height
swatch is unchanged. Runtime gradients are optical shading detail; they do not
add collision geometry or a measured physical displacement calibration.

The 2026-11-04 bandwidth candidate uses verified native 2K RGB8/roughness R8,
R16 displacement and matching native RGB8 OpenGL normals at the unchanged
physical repeat/height range. It supersedes the earlier 1K source; exact URLs,
checksums and packed bytes are in the current provenance manifests. The High
optical material ceiling is evaluated separately from geometry and page budgets.
Source mip/residual CPU memory increases; no measured speedup is claimed.
