The derived mud-litter-01.bin.gz contains a native 1024-square RGB8 sRGB reflectance,
R8 linear roughness and R16 relative-height swatch. There is no spatial
downsample; runtime mips filter linear RGB. The source 16-bit roughness is
normalized before conversion to
8-bit (never truncated/modulo-wrapped). The source displacement remains 16-bit.
All three maps share the same coordinates. No baked AO or lighting is imported.

The source page reports a 1.3 m swatch width. The 0.025 m displacement range
is an explicit authored optical prior; no calibrated height scale, Thessa
botany, sediment inventory, collision or water depth is claimed.

Reproduce with tools/worldgen-rocky/import_mud_litter.py SOURCE_DIR DESTINATION.
Exact URLs use https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/png/1k/
brown_mud_leaves_01/brown_mud_leaves_01_{diff,rough,disp}_1k.png.
Source SHA256 hashes, output hash and quantized means are recorded in the
adjacent mud-litter-01.provenance.json. Pillow/NumPy are offline tooling only.

The companion mud-litter-01.normal.bin.gz contains CC0 OpenGL normals from the
same Poly Haven scan. Exact URLs, checksums and processing are in
mud-litter-01.normal.provenance.json. Reproduce with
tools/worldgen-rocky/import_ground_normals.py. The color/roughness/height and normal swatches retain the same native 1K
lattice. Runtime gradients are optical shading detail; they do not
add collision geometry or a measured physical displacement calibration.
