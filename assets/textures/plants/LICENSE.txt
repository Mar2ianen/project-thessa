Fern 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
Rob Tuytel (scanning), Rico Cilliers (modeling).
Source 2k diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness maps. Affine extraction of one pinna;
normal tangent components rotate with the extraction basis. RGB edge padding is
retained. Runtime silhouettes are explicit geometry, without alpha masking.
The authored plant archetype rescales the optical patch; this is not a measured
reconstruction of extinct foliage. No lighting is baked into the imported PBR maps
by this importer. Source capture/de-lighting accuracy remains the source's limit.

Exact source and derived SHA256 identities and affine coordinates are recorded
in provenance.json. Reproduction: tools/worldgen-rocky/import_plant_materials.py.

Leaf base colour is an authored archetype: the importer applies linear RGB
albedo gains [2.6, 2.5, 1.8] and clamps to [0,1]. The provenance manifest records
this transformation and pins all source SHA256s. It is not calibrated source
photometry or a measured fossil-leaf spectrum. Geometry UV U follows pinna length.

Bark colour also retains an authored linear RGB gain [2.4, 3.6, 5.2],
clamped to [0,1]. To reproduce the published patch exactly, the importer first
quantizes the linear-light 2x downsample to sRGB8, decodes that intermediate,
and then applies the gain. The gain is an appearance prior, not measured
lichen cover, a measured bark spectrum or a biological reconstruction.
