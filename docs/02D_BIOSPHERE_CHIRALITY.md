# Project Thessa — Biosphere Chirality

> Working biological-canon document. This file defines the handedness relationship between Thessa-derived life and the Solar-System easter-egg biosphere, and the resulting colonization / food-economy implications.

## 1. Canon target

Thessa-derived life, including humans, uses **mirror biochemistry relative to terrestrial life**.

Working convention:

- Thessa proteins are built primarily from **D-amino acids**;
- Thessa nucleic-acid / carbohydrate backbone chemistry uses the opposite handedness from terrestrial life, i.e. an **L-sugar family** where terrestrial biochemistry predominantly uses D-sugars;
- terrestrial life retains ordinary Earth chirality: **L-amino acids + D-sugars**.

The intent is not to make Earth physically uninhabitable. Atmospheric gases, liquid water, minerals, salts, metals, bulk carbon, nitrogen and other achiral feedstocks remain normal resources. The incompatibility is biochemical rather than environmental.

---

## 2. Gameplay consequence — Earth is habitable but not a free food source

The Solar-System easter egg can therefore contain a biologically rich ~200 Ma Earth that is still an excellent colonization target without giving the player a mature edible biosphere for free.

Direct consequences:

- Earth air and water are usable after ordinary environmental treatment;
- terrestrial plants and animals are **not automatically nutritionally compatible** with Thessa-derived humans;
- proteins and carbohydrates cannot be assumed to enter Thessa metabolism simply because their elemental composition is correct;
- direct consumption of terrestrial biomass provides little useful nutrition and may cause digestive / metabolic problems depending on the molecule;
- some achiral or weakly stereospecific molecules can still be usable;
- industrial chemistry can hydrolyze terrestrial biomass and convert / racemize / resynthesize selected feedstocks into the required handedness;
- imported Thessa crops, cultured tissue, algae, fungi or synthetic-food systems remain strategically important during colonization.

Earth therefore begins as a **ready-made physical habitat plus an enormous chemical feedstock reservoir**, not as a ready-made agricultural economy.

---

## 3. Food-processing design

Do not model chirality as a binary `edible / poisonous` flag.

Preferred layers:

1. **Inorganic / achiral resources** — water, salts, CO2, O2, N2, many simple mineral nutrients: directly usable.
2. **Simple organic feedstocks** — some fatty acids and other achiral compounds may be separable and reusable.
3. **Chiral monomers** — require stereochemical separation or conversion before efficient metabolic use.
4. **Complex terrestrial biopolymers** — poor direct food; better treated as refinery input.
5. **Thessa-native / mirror agriculture** — normal food production once imported organisms or synthetic biology infrastructure is established.

This creates a useful progression distinction between:

- landing on a habitable world;
- surviving from carried stores;
- building a chemical food refinery;
- establishing a self-sustaining mirror biosphere.

---

## 4. Biosafety consequence

Opposite chirality strongly reduces ordinary molecular compatibility between the two biospheres, but it should **not** be treated as perfect universal immunity.

Design rule:

- most host-specific enzymes, receptors, parasites and pathogens should have severe compatibility barriers;
- terrestrial macromolecular toxins and pathogens should usually fail to interact normally with Thessa biology;
- small achiral molecules, physical irritants, environmental toxins, radiation, oxygen chemistry and other non-chiral hazards remain real;
- some molecules / enzymes can be weakly stereoselective or operate on achiral substrates, so exceptions are possible.

This gives cross-biosphere contact a strong firewall without making it magically consequence-free.

---

## 5. Narrative consequence

The chirality difference is evidence that Thessa life and Earth life do **not** share a recent biological origin despite both evolving complex carbon-water biochemistry.

It also reinforces the captured-Thessa origin hypothesis: a biosphere older than the present Asterion configuration can remain biologically distinct from the Solar-System easter egg and other independently evolved biospheres.

The discovery of Earth is therefore scientifically shocking for two independent reasons:

- complex life evolved independently in another stellar environment;
- it solved many similar biochemical problems using the opposite molecular handedness.

---

## 6. Canon-lock questions

Before biological canon is frozen:

1. decide whether the mirror relation applies to the complete information system (proteins + nucleic acids + major sugars) or only the dominant nutritional pathways;
2. define which achiral terrestrial nutrients Thessa humans can use directly;
3. define whether gut symbionts are also mirror-biochemistry organisms;
4. define industrial conversion efficiency for Earth biomass -> Thessa-compatible food feedstock;
5. decide whether any independent non-Thessa biospheres in the playable cloud use Earth chirality, Thessa chirality, or a third biochemical solution;
6. keep the mechanic primarily about logistics / ecology rather than turning every meal into a stereochemistry minigame.
