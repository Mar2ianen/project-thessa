The embedded glTF is the existing B02 core-cutout-v1 conversion of the CC0
fern_02 1k Blender asset. It contains four scanned botanical growth forms,
base colour with alpha, roughness and tangent-space normal maps. The conversion
record is fern-02-conversion.json. Original source shader translucency/mix/hue
nodes are not reproduced; native leaf transmission is an authored parameter.
Native procedural crown placement/warping is a presentation derivative, not
an identified fossil species or a botanical growth simulation.
No reference photograph or generated target image is projected onto the scene.
