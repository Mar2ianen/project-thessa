# Albedo (base color, unlit)

Natural daylight base color, sRGB, flat lighting, no shadows/AO:

- rock `#8A7B6B`, sand `#D9B380`, highlands `#6B6B6B`
- basalt/volcanic `#3A2A26`, fresh lava `#FF4A00` (only inside volcanic biome)
- ice/snow `#E8F4FF`, deep water `#0B2A5B`, shallow water `#2E7FD9`
- iron-rich soil `#9A5A3C`, sulfur tint `#C9B458` (rare, near vents)

No clouds, no night side, no specular highlights. Must follow height shapes.
